Frequently asked questions

My notes disappeared after restoring a backup. What can I do? expand_more

Restoring in JW Library replaces all data on the device. If you still have older backups, merge them into one with Kovetz and restore that combined file.

Does it require an internet connection? expand_more

No. Internet is only needed to download the app and, optionally, to check for new versions.

Is it safe for my existing backups? expand_more

Yes. Kovetz never modifies your source files and always writes a new output file. Still, make a fresh backup of your device before restoring.

What happens if I edited the same note on two devices? expand_more

The newer version is kept in the merged backup, while the other version is saved in the merge report so you can review it.

Can I import notes back into JW Library from PDF or Obsidian? expand_more

No — PDF and Obsidian exports are for reading and searching. To bring notes back into JW Library, restore the merged .jwlibrary backup.

Is this an official JW Library app? expand_more

No. Kovetz is an independent open-source helper (MIT license) and is not affiliated with JW Library or its developers.
